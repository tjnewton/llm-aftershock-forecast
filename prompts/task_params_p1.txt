## Task
Do not report counts. Instead, estimate the parameters of the Reasenberg-Jones aftershock model
for this sequence. In that model the rate of aftershocks with magnitude M or larger at time t
days after the mainshock (magnitude Mm) is

    rate(t, M) = 10^(a + b * (Mm - M)) * (t + c)^(-p)   per day.

Mm is the mainshock magnitude given above, M is a catalog magnitude, and a is defined together
with your b. We will compute the expected count in each window by integrating this rate, and the
full forecast distribution from your uncertainty on a. Give:
- b: the Gutenberg-Richter b-value;
- p and c (c in days): the Omori-Utsu decay parameters;
- a_p05, a_p50, a_p95: the 5th, 50th and 95th percentiles of your uncertainty on a.
