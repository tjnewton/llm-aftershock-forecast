## What to report
Report model parameters, not counts. We use the Reasenberg-Jones model, where aftershocks of
magnitude at least M occur at a rate of

    10^(a + b * (Mm - M)) * (t + c)^(-p)   events per day

at t days after a mainshock of magnitude Mm (the magnitude given above); a is defined together
with your b. Code will integrate the rate over each window and turn your uncertainty in a into a
forecast distribution. Report:
1. b, the Gutenberg-Richter b-value;
2. p, the Omori-Utsu decay exponent, and c, its time offset in days;
3. a_p05, a_p50 and a_p95, your 5th, 50th and 95th percentile values for the productivity a.
